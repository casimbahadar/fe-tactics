Design notes

Inherited from FETO

Growth is authored, not rolled. You spend a budget of base points and growth percentages when a soldier joins, and their whole career follows from it: 50% growth is one point every two levels, exactly, forever. Twenty growth ticks before promotion, nineteen after. Stats cap at 20 then 30; HP at 40 then 60. Anything above a cap is lost.

What we changed

Respec is free between battles. The original punished you for not knowing the maths before you'd played. Caps still matter, but now they are a puzzle you can re-solve instead of a mistake you live with.

Weapon rank rises by use, not by battles survived.

Every chapter tells you what it tests before you deploy, so allocation is a decision rather than a guess.

Score Run1 player, one battle
Points: win +1000, each kill +50, each turn taken −20, each soldier lost −150.
Gauntlet1 player, five battles
One squad of six fights five battles that get harder each time, with no healing in between. Damage taken and weapon use carry over. Soldiers who die stay dead for the rest of the run. Experience they earn counts for the run only.
Points: each battle won +500, all five won +1000, each kill +50, each turn taken −20, each soldier lost −150.
Split Field2 players, same device
Two identical squads fight the same group of enemies on one map, taking turns on this device. Whoever scores higher wins. When the enemies have to choose between two equal targets, they go for the squad that has damaged them most, then the one with the least health left, then whichever squad the turn number picks.
Points: each enemy killed +50, each soldier lost −150. You can attack the other squad, but killing one of their soldiers costs you 100 points. Highest score wins once the enemies are wiped out, or at turn 15.
Hold the Point2 players, same device
A fort sits in the middle of the map, guarded by enemies. You score a hold each time your turn ends with one of your soldiers standing on the fort, alive. Three holds wins outright. Stepping off, or losing the soldier standing there, freezes your count where it is: get back on the fort and it carries on from there.
If nobody reaches three holds by turn 15, whoever has the most holds wins, and if that is tied, the higher score does. Killing the other squad's soldiers still costs 100 points each.
Team Battle2 to 6 players, no computer army
Squads only, split into two sides. Squads on your side cannot hurt you and your scores add together. Win by clearing the other side off the map, or by holding the higher score at turn 20.
Format points: kill +50, own soldier lost −150
Online2 to 6 players, one each
Play Split Field or Hold the Point across separate devices: phones, tablets, laptops, any mix. One player opens a room and shares the code, everyone else joins with it. All of you see the same map and the same rolls.
